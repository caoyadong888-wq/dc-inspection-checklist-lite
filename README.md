# Daily DC Walkthrough Checklist (lite)

## English

A free slice of the daily walk: power, cooling, and the cage door from the DC-A hall H1 sample on 2026-03-18. Eight filled rows, not the full twenty.

## Purpose

Show the severity words and the shape of a filled walk without the workbook, the fire-panel section, or the CSV exporter.

## Deliverables

- `deliverables/walkthrough_short.md` — power, CRAC supply, and door checks, with the readings from the sample morning.
- `deliverables/severity_scale_short.md` — the four severity words and the three Critical items those eight rows can see.
- `examples/sample_day_short.csv` — the same eight rows in the full column layout.

## Quick start

Open `deliverables/walkthrough_short.md` and read W-02, W-06, and W-15. Those three are the Fail rows in this slice. W-06 and W-15 are Critical.

The short CSV is not a full day. It has no humidity, aisle, cable, fire-panel, or spare-parts rows. The full exporter expects all eight areas.
